# Voice and AI-slop elimination

The fastest way to lose a reader (and to get ignored by an AI assistant trying to
quote a credible source) is to sound like a machine wrote it. This is the scan the
skill runs on every draft before output. Run it on anything you write by hand too.

## The core test

Read it aloud in your head. If it sounds like a corporate memo, a motivational
poster, or a LinkedIn thought-leader with nothing to say, rewrite it. Real writing
has a point. Every sentence earns its place. If you can delete a sentence and lose
nothing, delete it.

## Dead-giveaway words — never use

landscape, leverage, navigate, delve, unpack, robust, comprehensive, holistic,
realm, foster, tapestry, elevate, resonate, empower, seamless, harness, curate,
pivotal, paramount, myriad, crucially, notably, importantly, "at its core",
"in essence", furthermore, moreover, additionally, "that said", synergies,
actionable, stakeholders, agile, resilient, "in today's [X]",
"in an ever-changing [X]", "this signals", "this underscores",
"quietly underscores", "powerfully demonstrates", game-changer, unlock,
revolutionary, disrupt, skyrocket.

| AI slop | What a person says |
|---|---|
| "Delve into" / "Unpack" | "Look at" / "Break down" |
| "Navigate the complexities of" | Name the actual problem |
| "This signals / underscores" | State the opinion plainly |
| "In an ever-changing landscape" | Say what's actually changing, or cut it |
| "Leverage our learnings" | "Here's what worked and what didn't" |
| "Holistic approach" | Say the actual approach |
| "Actionable next steps" | Just list the steps |

## Dead-giveaway formatting — never do

1. **Transition-word openers.** Never start a sentence or bullet with Moreover,
   Furthermore, Additionally, That said, Importantly, Notably, Crucially, In
   addition. If you wouldn't say it out loud, don't write it.
2. **Bold-word-colon-explanation bullets.** This is the single most recognizable
   AI tell:
   - ❌ "**Clarity:** Make sure your post is clear."
   - ✅ "Cut anything a reader has to reread."
3. **The neat little bow.** No generic wrap-ups ("At the end of the day, it comes
   down to execution"). If your closing line could end any article on earth, it
   says nothing. Make it specific or cut it.
4. **Says-everything-means-nothing paragraphs.** If you can't state the point of a
   paragraph in one plain sentence, delete the paragraph.

## Dead-giveaway sentence structures — never use

- **Forced negation / the reframe:** "It's not X, it's Y." "Instead of X, Y." AI
  loves this. Humans just state the thing.
- **Staccato repetition:** "Not this, or this, or this, but that."
- **Excessive adverbs:** "quietly underscores", "powerfully demonstrates",
  "seamlessly integrates". Just say what happened.
- **Mirrored parallel structures** for fake symmetry.
- **Triple buildup** that escalates for drama when one item would do.
- **Rhetorical question then immediate answer** as a crutch.
- **"Let me break this down."** Opening with **"Imagine."** Alliterative lists of
  three. Bookend/callback closes.

## Em dashes

No em dashes. Anywhere. Not in posts, not in captions, not in headings. Use
periods, commas, or line breaks. This one is absolute.

## What good sounds like

- Short sentences. Varied rhythm. Lopsided and asymmetric, like speech.
- Concrete over abstract. A number or example beats an adjective.
- Founder-to-founder, peer level. Not a guru, not a needy vendor.
- Contractions are fine (you'll, we've, here's).
- Confident, specific, a little dry. Says one true thing per paragraph.

When you find a banned pattern, don't soften it. Rewrite the sentence to say the
real thing in plain words.
